## 🛡️ App Privacy & Data Safety Declarations

### iOS App Store Nutrition Labels
- **Data Not Linked to You**:
  - **Usage Data**: Product Interaction (opt-in anonymous telemetry only, disabled by default).
  - **Diagnostics**: Crash logs.
- **Data Used to Track You**:
  - **None**. Foobow does not track users across third-party apps or websites.
- **Data Stored Locally on Device**:
  - Daily thoughts, journal reflections, streak counts, wooden fish taps, and senior readability preferences are stored in device-local encrypted storage (`AsyncStorage`).

### Google Play Data Safety
- **Data Collected & Shared**:
  - **Personal Info**: Optional Email / Username if signing in via Clerk (used solely for account access; guest mode available without sign-in).
  - **Financial Info**: Purchase/donation receipts (processed directly through verified processors like Stripe; Foobow does not store credit card numbers).
  - **Data Encryption**: All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  - **Account Deletion**: Users can request account and data deletion directly from the Profile screen.
